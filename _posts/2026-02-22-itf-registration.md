Do you have a student who is currently in 6th - 12th grade who loves theatre? Do they want to see all the plays coming to town, be in all the shows and just do ALL things theatre? The International Thespian Festival may be just the thing for them! This is an annual event that is held at the Indiana University Bloomington campus from June 21st-26th \(travel days may add a day on either side\). For more info about the event check out [the website](https://itf.schooltheatre.org/index.cfm0). For questions regarding Willows students traveling feel free to email [Ms. Becky](mailto:bgil@willowsprep.com). If you are ready to register your student, please fill out [the ITF Registration Form](https://rpv5m9hbb.cc.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001fymoJWpufI-O8e2nslCsjuCEVew_ew5zfO5bdo1ixF_W9fWjJ3O1IM2zQr4-OkcTdwiLBTN5ZZiDyrfPrnfWFNF4CrJ8Ysf_5SGXNox9sVKYAHvk-391HJ8wLaslNTYr7d76dSHEOkqzbMUKBovDdgcyLS7xeLlbsCCmQxfG5hU=&c=0fzEYgESc-pWr-WnxvdXH1EUKjcgItvTxV7daB51Y2uUJnagqSxcOg==&ch=ww_SYdYL7DzXHoyBxIzKcmGDKn9CL-85tBh2Dn1KePiNJ7jhE95qlA==) \(info on cost at the top of the form\).
