It's time for our spring play: The SpongeBob Musical!  
  
The entire play is open to all students - high school or middle school! And yes, the entire play: All roles are open to ALL BODIES – age and gender are not part of the casting conversation (because let’s face it, if you picked up a sea sponge – or a squirrel - could YOU tell if it was a girl or a boy or how old it was?). The exception to this is if two actors are equally matched for a role, age or experience may be the tiebreaker.  
  
Tech Roles are also available!
