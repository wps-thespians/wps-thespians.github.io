# Security Policy

Hmmm... I'm not a lawyer, and I don't wanna go get one. But if you find something bad in the site, please tell me. I will do my absolute best to address it ASAP.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please use GitHub's vulnerability reporting feature to disclose vulnerabilities. If you can, please ALSO tell me how to fix it - I am bad at this!

If you are really worried and think I need to see it ASAP, email our troupe director, Ms. Becky, at bgil@willowsprep.com, and just tell them to forward it to "the webmaster of the website" - she'll know who that is.
